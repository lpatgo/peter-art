"I took Intro to Drawing with Peter Mandradjieff at PCAM. Peter is an outstanding teacher, and the class revolutionized my approach to drawing. Peter explained and introduced concepts clearly, brought in outside materials when necessary, and encouraged a repetition-based, hands-on approach to instruction. Before the class, I had never once used charcoal to draw. As a result of this 8-week class I feel like I developed a high degree of competency in the medium. Absolutely the best art class I have ever taken."  -Grant Catton, art student
