"I took Intro to Drawing with Peter Mandradjieff at PCAM. Peter is an outstanding teacher, and the class revolutionized my approach to drawing. Peter explained and introduced concepts clearly, brought in outside materials when necessary, and encouraged a repetition-based, hands-on approach to instruction. Before the class, I had never once used charcoal to draw. As a result of this 8-week class I feel like I developed a high degree of competency in the medium. Absolutely the best art class I have ever taken."  -Grant Catton, art student

"I took Peter's painting class this summer at Pittsburgh Center for Arts & Media and learned so much. His teaching style was both professional and inspiring, and the topics he introduced challenged me creatively while helping me grow into a more confident painter.

Peter has a natural gift for connecting with each student and fostering their individual potential. He created a calm, encouraging environment that made it easy to experiment, ask questions, and develop new skills. His thoughtful guidance and genuine passion for painting were evident in every class.

It was both a pleasure and an honor to learn from Peter. Instructors with his talent, generosity, and ability to inspire others are truly invaluable. I highly recommend his classes to anyone looking to grow as an artist, regardless of their level of experience."  -Michelle DellaCorte, art student
